Zekken Biography

Zachary “Zekken” Patrone is a professional VALORANT player from the United States of America. He is known for his aggressive and explosive playstyle, especially on Duelist agents. Zekken has competed at the highest level of VALORANT and has become one of the well-known players in the professional scene. He is currently listed by ProSettings as a player for MIBR. His current setup includes a Razer Viper V4 Pro Black mouse, MelGeek MADE68 Ultra V2 keyboard, ZOWIE XL2586X+ monitor, and Razer BlackShark V3 Pro Black headset.
