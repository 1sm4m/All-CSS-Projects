aspas Biography

Erick “aspas” Santos is a professional VALORANT player from Brazil who is currently playing for MIBR. He first rose to prominence in the VALORANT scene while playing for LOUD. Upon joining LOUD's roster in February 2022, he quickly became one of the most popular and highly regarded players in the game. While playing for LOUD, aspas won VALORANT Champions 2022 after defeating OpTic Gaming 3-1 in the Grand Final in Istanbul. He is especially known for his explosive Duelist play, precision aim, fast reactions, and aggressive style.
