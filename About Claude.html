About Claude

Claude is an AI assistant created by Anthropic. It can answer questions, help with coding, writing, learning, analysis, and many other tasks. It understands natural language and provides thoughtful, human-like responses.

Features of Claude

  • Answers questions on many topics.
  • Helps with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and programming.
  • Can write essays, emails, and reports.
  • Explains difficult concepts in simple language.
  • Supports creative writing and brainstorming.
  • Available for education, business, and personal use.

Anthropic Claude Models

  • Claude 1 – The first Claude model, focused on safe and helpful conversation.
  • Claude 2 – Improved reasoning and longer context handling.
  • Claude 3 – A family of models (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus) balancing speed and intelligence.
  • Claude 3.5 – Faster and more capable, widely used for coding and conversation.
  • Claude 4 – Improved reasoning, creativity, and accuracy across text and images.
  • Claude Sonnet 4.5 – Optimized for coding, instruction following, and long tasks.
  • Claude Haiku 4.5 – A fast, lightweight model for quick everyday tasks.
  • Claude Opus 4.8 – A newer generation focused on stronger reasoning and complex tasks.
  • Claude Sonnet 5 – Balanced performance and speed for everyday use.
  • Claude Fable 5 / Claude Mythos 5 – Anthropic's newest tier, with Mythos 5 offered through a limited early-access program.

Conclusion

Claude models continue to improve over time, making AI more helpful for students, developers, businesses, and everyday users around the world.
